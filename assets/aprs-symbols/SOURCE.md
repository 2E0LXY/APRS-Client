APRS map symbols are vendored from the aprs.fi high-resolution APRS symbol set:

https://github.com/hessu/aprs-symbols

The APRS symbol table itself is documented by APRS.org:

https://www.aprs.org/symbols.html

The PNG sprites in this folder are the 24px @2x primary, secondary and overlay tables from hessu/aprs-symbols.
The upstream copyright and licensing notes are included in COPYRIGHT.md.
